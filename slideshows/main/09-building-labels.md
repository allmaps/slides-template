These points mark historic buildings recorded by the City of Amsterdam. Each
label reads the building's name from the GeoJSON's `Naam` property. Zoom in to
see more labels where buildings are close together.

The previous slide's transport routes disappear here: each slide starts from
the global layer defaults.
