This narrative map was made by Jules Schoonman as a [template](https://github.com/allmaps/slides-template) to get started using [Allmaps Slides](https://github.com/allmaps/slides). It is licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

The map originates from the [Allard Pierson](https://hdl.handle.net/11245/3.39844) collection (University of Amsterdam). The high resolution image is loaded from static tiles served through [this repository](https://github.com/amsterdamtimemachine/berckenrode-iiif).

The GeoJSON overlays use City of Amsterdam open data: [tram and metro routes](https://maps.amsterdam.nl/open_geodata/?k=648) and [historic buildings](https://maps.amsterdam.nl/open_geodata/geojson_lnglat.php?KAARTLAAG=HISTORISCHE_BEBOUWING&THEMA=archeologie).
